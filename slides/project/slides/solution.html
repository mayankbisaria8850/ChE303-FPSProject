Flows from the Aspen base case: 1,000 kg/h of wet pomace, 1.0 mm screen cut size.

ZestLoop · Company and problem

Each unit uses a Fluid Particle Systems principle. The mill reduces particle size so juice is released, the screen classifies by size, the decanter does centrifugal solid-liquid separation, and the dryer removes the remaining moisture. At the 1 tonne per hour design point the plant makes 167 kg/h of dry fibre, 449 kg/h of juice and 73 kg/h of coarse peel (32.5 kg/h solids plus 40 kg/h of entrained liquid).
