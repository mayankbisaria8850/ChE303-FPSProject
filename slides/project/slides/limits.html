CONCLUSIONS

Limitations and next steps

Limitations

  • Valid only for 500–2,000 kg/h and 0.5–1.5 mm, with a fixed 80/20 feed and PSD.
  • Wᵢ = 12 and α = 3.5 are typical values, not measured on our pomace.
  • Dryer air (1,500 kg/h at 90 °C) is not sized for the ~280 kg/h water load.

Future improvements

  • Recycle 90 % of the oversize to the mill: fibre recovery from 84 % to over 97 %.
  • Size the dryer from a heat balance and the decanter from Σ-theory.
  • Measure Wᵢ, the screen curve and cake moisture on real pomace.

ZestLoop · Conclusions

Be open about the limits. The models are only valid inside the simulated window. The work index and screen sharpness are default or literature-type values. The dryer is a shortcut model: evaporating about 280 kg/h of water needs roughly 185 kW, while 1500 kg/h of air cooled from 90 to 30 °C supplies only about 25 kW, so the air flow or a heat duty must be sized properly. Aspen also sends dissolved sugar and acid out with the exhaust. Next steps: an oversize recycle loop (the Aspen report estimates fibre recovery above 97 percent), proper dryer and decanter sizing, and lab measurements on real pomace.
