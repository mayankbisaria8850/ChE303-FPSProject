RESULT AT d₅₀ = 1.0 mm, α = 3.5

ZestLoop · Python equipment calculations

The Whiten selection function gives the fraction of particles of size d that report to the oversize. x is the particle size divided by the cut size, alpha is the sharpness; at d equal to the cut size, E is exactly one half. We weight E by the mass fraction in each milled size class, using the class midpoint as its size. At a 1.0 mm cut, 16.26 percent of the solids go to oversize, so 32.52 kg/h of the 200 kg/h of cellulose, exactly the Aspen value. Alpha = 3.5 is Aspen's default; we identified it because it reproduces all 42 OVERSIZE values. Assumptions: partition by size only, no breakage on the deck, 5 percent of the liquid carried into the oversize. Code: calc/screen.py.
