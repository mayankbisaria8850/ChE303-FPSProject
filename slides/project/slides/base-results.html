ZestLoop · Aspen simulation results

This is the Aspen base case: 1000 kg/h feed and a 1.0 mm screen. Inputs (pomace plus hot air, 2500 kg/h) equal outputs (oversize, juice, product and exhaust). 83.7 percent of the cellulose ends up as dry fibre; the rest is coarse peel in the oversize. The decanter recovers 56 percent of the liquid as clear juice; the rest leaves in the 65 percent moisture cake and is evaporated in the dryer. The mill draws 0.615 kW. Limitation to mention: Aspen sends the dissolved glucose and citric acid out with the exhaust, which a real dryer would not do.
