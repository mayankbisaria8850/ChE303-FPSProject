The engineering trade-off: a wider screen lets more fibre through, so fibre product rises by half and oversize falls. But every extra kilogram of fibre that reaches the decanter leaves in a 65 percent moisture cake, carrying about 1.86 kg of liquid with it, so clarified juice falls by about a fifth. Mill power doesn't change because the mill outlet size distribution is fixed. The customer chooses the cut size by which product is worth more to them.
