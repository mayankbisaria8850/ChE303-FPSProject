ZestLoop · Aspen simulation results

We varied two inputs on a full grid: total feed at 7 levels and screen cut size at 6 levels, 42 converged cases. Feed is the condition we cannot control (seasonal fruit supply); cut size is the main design knob, because the centrifuge keeps every milled particle and the dryer dries everything it gets, so the screen alone decides how much fibre becomes product. Below 0.5 mm sticky pectin slurries would blind the mesh; above 1.5 mm all milled fibre already passes. The chart is at 1000 kg/h; the star marks the base case at 1.0 mm, which is not one of the 42 points.
