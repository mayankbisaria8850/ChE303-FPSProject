ZestLoop · Live website demonstration

Start the site before the talk (streamlit run app/app.py) and keep the browser open on the Calculator tab. Step 1 shows an exact lookup of a simulated case. Step 2 shows the trade-off: fibre rises, juice falls, because every extra kilogram of fibre leaves the decanter carrying about 1.86 kg of liquid. Step 3 shows linear scale-up: recoveries stay the same, flows double. Step 4 shows the Python model: Bond's law with a metric-tonne basis drops 9.3 percent below Aspen; a sharper screen passes more fine fibre. Step 5 shows that the site states its valid range and how accurate the interpolation is. Also point to the Process tab, where the flowsheet labels update live.
