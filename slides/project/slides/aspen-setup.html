Why each block, and how it was set up

NRTL property method

Water, sugar and citric acid form a strongly polar mixture; NRTL captures the liquid non-ideality.

MIXCIPSD stream class

Tracks the liquid and a 10-interval solid PSD (0–10 mm) in every stream.

SCALE-FD calculator

Sets solids = 0.25 × liquid at every sweep point, keeping the feed at 80/20.

ZestLoop · Aspen flowsheet

Property set-up: NRTL because the liquid is a polar water, sugar and acid mixture; MIXCIPSD so Aspen tracks both the liquid and the particle size distribution of the cellulose; a Calculator block keeps the 80 to 20 liquid to solid ratio when the feed is varied. Block order: mill first to release juice and make the feed pumpable, screen before the centrifuge to protect it, centrifuge before the dryer because mechanical dewatering is far cheaper than evaporation. Note for questions: Aspen applies the Bond work index per short ton (Bond's original definition), and the screen used Aspen's default Whiten sharpness of 3.5.
