ZestLoop · Python equipment calculations

Governing equation: Bond's third law. Variables: W specific energy in kWh per ton of solids, Wi the work index (12 kWh per ton for fibrous peel), F80 and P80 the 80 percent passing sizes of feed and product in micrometres, and the solids mass flow. The hand calculation interpolates F80 and P80 from the cumulative PSD tables, then gives 0.6146 kW, matching Aspen to nine significant figures. The independent check is that the Python function reproduces all seven Aspen MILL_POWER values. Finding: the match only works with Bond's original short-ton basis, so Aspen treats Wi as kWh per short ton. Code: calc/mill.py, tests in calc/tests/test_mill.py.
