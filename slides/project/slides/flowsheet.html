Base case, all flows in kg/h · steady state, open loop · Aspen Plus V14 with NRTL and the MIXCIPSD stream class.

ZestLoop · Aspen flowsheet

Walk the flowsheet left to right. Feed: 1000 kg/h of pomace at 25 °C, 800 kg/h liquid (water, glucose, citric acid) and 200 kg/h cellulose. The mill shreds peel from F80 5.7 mm to P80 0.75 mm. The screen at 1.0 mm sends 72.5 kg/h of coarse peel to oversize and 927.5 kg/h of slurry on. The decanter recovers 449 kg/h of clear juice and a 478.5 kg/h cake at 65 percent moisture. The dryer uses 1500 kg/h of air at 90 °C to give 167.5 kg/h of dry fibre. Product, waste or co-product, and intermediate streams are all shown.
